Published research

Compartmental Models for Detecting Fake News Propagation in OSN with Variable Population

V. Nithish Kumar, G. Praneeth Kumar, Sujoy Datta, Santosh Kumar Uppada, B. Sivaselvan

Springer, Communications in Computer and Information Science vol. 2732 (ICICCT 2025), pp. 185–196 · Published 10 Jan 2026

Modified epidemic models that predict how fake news moves through online social networks. The paper solves the differential equations, computes the basic reproduction number and simulates the spread across six states:

  • Susceptible
  • Exposed
  • Infected
  • Recovered
  • Deceased
  • Skeptical

Read on Springer DOI 10.1007/978-3-032-08638-9_14
